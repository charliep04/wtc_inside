The purpose of this webpage is to index and showcase all known photographs taken within the World Trade Center complex on the morning of September 11, 2001, both before and during the attacks. The page currently hosts 90 photographs taken by 17 individuals that morning. It has been a quarter of a century since that fateful day, and I believe it is now more important than ever to preserve these photographs before they are lost to time.
